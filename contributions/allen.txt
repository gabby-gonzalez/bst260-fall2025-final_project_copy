# Reflection
This was a very fun project overall. Although I have interacted with Shiny apps before, I have never made one myself. This project therefore created a perfect opportunity to design such an app, in a supported environment with feedback from the teaching associates and my peers. In addition to the mechanistic task of creating the app itself (and all the debugging/programming associated), it made me reflect on the importance of good data layout and plot design; the Shiny app also added the extra dimension of figuring out what sort of interactive tools are best to really capitalise on Shiny's potential. I also very much enjoyed working with my team, all of whom were very collaborative and all contributed equally to the final product.
