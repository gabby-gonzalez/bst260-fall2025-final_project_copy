#Dataset to be used: NHANES (2009-2012)

#Research questions:
How can we use RShiny to help visualize trends and associations of lifestyle patterns among individuals included in the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) data set in the United States between 2009-2012?


#Analysis plan:
Good data visualization is an important tool for identifying and summarizing associations and interactions within health-related data. We propose to develop an interactive data exploration application using RShiny, which will allow users to dynamically explore relationships between lifestyle patterns and health indicators in the NHANES dataset. The RShiny app will integrate data visualization, summary statistics, and customizable filtering options to facilitate exploration of population-level associations. 

In order to accomplish this, our analysis will include identifying relevant lifestyle and health indicator variables within NHANES, cleaning and wrangling data, exploring missing data and mitigation strategies as needed (eg., complete case analysis versus imputation method), and finally presenting relevant plots and summary statistics within the RShiny application. Important covariates such as age, sex, race/ethnicity, education, and socioeconomic status will be included for potential stratification of patterns across different subgroups. Appropriate visualization strategies will be chosen to account for different variable types (eg., continuous, categorical, binary, time trends, etc.), and visualization techniques such as measures of spread and smoothing may be incorporated where necessary.

#Variables of interest (initial, might be updated later):
Lifestyle variables: SleepHrsNight, PhysActive, AlcoholDay, AlcoholYear, SmokeNow, SmokeNow, Smoke100, Smoke100, HardDrugs
Indicators of health: BMI, BPSysAve, BPDiaAve, TotChol, Diabetes
Covariates: Gender, Age, AgeDecade, Race1, Education, HHIncome
